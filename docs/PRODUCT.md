# Product brief

## Problem

Access to legal help is often blocked before a person ever reaches a lawyer: they do not know how to classify the issue, which facts matter, what evidence to collect, or which official channel to contact. Displacement, deadlines, accessibility needs, and immediate safety concerns make that first step harder.

## User

A person in Ukraine who has a civil, administrative, employment, documentation, benefits, housing, or safety-related problem and needs a trustworthy first route in plain language.

## MVP promise

In under three minutes, turn an unstructured account into:

- an urgency-aware first-action route;
- a short evidence checklist;
- a verified official support contact;
- a structured draft for the first consultation.

## Design principles

1. **Start with the person's words.** Legal vocabulary is not required.
2. **Safety before paperwork.** Immediate risk changes the first action.
3. **Collect less.** Names, addresses, and document numbers are explicitly discouraged in the prototype.
4. **Show provenance.** Public contacts link directly to their official source.
5. **No false authority.** Navigation is clearly separated from legal advice.

## Production path

A responsible production version would add expert-reviewed Ukrainian legal playbooks, source citations per recommendation, update monitoring, accessibility testing, secure case handoff, user consent, data minimization, and human review for high-risk cases.
